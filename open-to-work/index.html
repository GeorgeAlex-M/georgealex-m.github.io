2.Let the day run

Try 3am. That is when most of this got made.

The house is the day job. It has been a good house — six years of software testing, Amber and then Electronic Arts, ISTQB Foundation and Agile, CompTIA. Its lights go off at a sensible hour.

The shed is the other thing. It is where the telescope on the bench came from, and the guitar, and the little board with the LED still blinking on it. It is where fifty physics simulations got written, and Ghidra MCP, and Genome Blueprint, and a book called Metathoughts that is still being written. Nobody assigned any of it. I wanted it to exist, so I built it after the shift ended, which is why the shed light is the last one burning in every frame after about eleven.

The gate has been open a while. That is the only part of this drawing that is a request.
